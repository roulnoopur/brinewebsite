BRINE is an eleven-course tasting menu restaurant on The Shore in Leith, Edinburgh. The kitchen operates an on-site vertical hydroponic farm, a live fermentation and preservation programme, and a 45-day dry-ageing programme. Every ingredient is sourced within 50 miles of Edinburgh.
