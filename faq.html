FAQ

No. BRINE operates by reservation only. We do not accept walk-ins at any time. Reservations can be made via our reservations page.

There is no published menu. BRINE serves a single eleven-course tasting menu that changes with the season, the harvest, and what is ready in the fermentation larder. Dietary requirements are accommodated — please let us know at the time of booking.

The eleven-course tasting menu is £105 per head. Drinks are not included. A wine pairing (£60), cocktail pairing (£70), cheese board supplement (£18), and coffee & petit fours (£15) are available to add at the time of booking or on the night.

Yes. We accommodate most dietary requirements with advance notice. Please include full details — including allergies, intolerances, and preferences — when making your reservation. We are unable to accommodate requirements disclosed on the night without prior notice.

A deposit is taken per person at the time of booking to secure your reservation. Cancellations made more than 72 hours before your reservation will receive a full refund of the deposit. Cancellations made within 72 hours are non-refundable. To amend or cancel a reservation, please contact us directly via reservations@brineleith.co.uk.

BRINE is available for exclusive hire on Tuesdays. Please visit our Private Bookings page to submit an enquiry. We aim to respond to all enquiries within 48 hours.
