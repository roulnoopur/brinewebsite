About

The Concept

BRINE is an eleven-course tasting menu restaurant on The Shore in Leith, Edinburgh, operating across Wednesday to Sunday, with an exclusive Tuesday buyout for corporate and private events. The kitchen operates an on-site vertical hydroponic farm, a live fermentation and preservation programme, and a 45-day dry-ageing programme — all working in service of a single menu at £105 per head. Every element of the operation is designed to make the food's provenance tangible to the guest.

The Philosophy

  • Complete Transparency
  • Premium quality at an accessible price
  • Dining that tells a story
  • Provenance visible in the room
