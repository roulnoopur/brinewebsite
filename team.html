The Team

Chef & Founder

Noopur Roul

Noopur Roul is the chef and founder of BRINE. With a background spanning UK and international fine dining kitchens, she founded BRINE to build a restaurant rooted in provenance, fermentation and craft — where the food's origin is visible in the room.

BRINE is a small, dedicated team. Every dish, every service, every ferment is made in this building.
